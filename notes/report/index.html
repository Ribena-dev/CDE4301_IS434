Fabrication of a Grid Resolution Standard via Proton-Beam Writing

Devinaa Kumeresh · AY2025

A0266490X

Abstract

Calibration of high-resolution electron microscopy systems requires physical reference standards with well-defined, near-vertical feature geometry. This project explores the use of Proton Beam Writing (PBW) to fabricate grid resolution standards. By leveraging the low lateral straggle of high-energy protons, we aim to achieve sidewall angles exceeding 89 degrees and surface roughness values in the sub-nanometer regime.
