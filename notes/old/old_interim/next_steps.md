## 6. Next Steps and plans

More work comparing and quantifying various metal compositions and thicknesses is needed. Given current system settings, gold is clearly not suitable; different metal like palladium with higher atomic mass, good conductivity, and better sputtering system compatibility may be tried.

As mentioned previously, trying different proton beam writing patterns focusing on lower dosages and more passes is priority. Finally, converging beam and focal plane placement awaits testing. More calculations of internal beam path and lens are required to find optimal focal plane placement given machine 5 nm accuracy.
