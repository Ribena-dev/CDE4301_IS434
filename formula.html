$$I(x) = A \cdot \text{erf}\left(\frac{x - x_0}{\sigma}\right) + B \cdot \exp\left(-\frac{(x - x_0)^2}{2\sigma^2}\right) + C$$
where:
  • $$I(x) = \text{intensity at position} x$$
  • $$A = \text{error function amplitude (edge transition sharpness) }$$
  • $$\text{erf} = \text{error function representing edge transition}$$
  • $$x_0 = \text{edge position}$$
  • $$\sigma = \text{standard deviation related to beam width}$$
  • $$B = \text{Gaussian amplitude (beam tail effects)}$$
  • $$C = \text{baseline intensity}$$
